# 🚴 Adventure Works Retail Analytics
### End-to-End EDA | Python | Pandas | Matplotlib | Seaborn

---

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on Microsoft's 
Adventure Works dataset — a global retail business selling bikes, 
components, clothing and accessories across 6 countries.

**Dataset:** Microsoft Adventure Works Sales Data  
**Records:** 1,21,253 transactions | 7 linked tables | 3 years (2017–2020)  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Power BI

## 🔍 Key Findings (So Far)
- 🚴 **Bikes drive 86.2%** of total revenue ($94.6M out of $109.8M)
- 🌍 **6 countries** — USA, Canada, UK, France, Australia, Germany
- 💰 **Average Profit Margin: 30.3%** — healthy retail performance
- 🏪 **Perfect 50-50 split** between B2B Resellers and B2C Internet sales
- 🏆 **Mountain-200** series dominates — 6 of top 10 revenue products
